What We Stand For

Built on three pillars.

Our commitment to quality, heritage, and responsibility shapes every decision we make — from sourcing to packaging.

Uncompromising Quality

Every piece is crafted from BIS-hallmarked 925 sterling silver — rigorously tested, hypoallergenic, and built to retain its brilliance for years. We don't cut corners, because neither should your jewels.

Artisan Crafted

Each design is brought to life by master artisans blending centuries-old hand techniques with precision modern finishing. No two pieces are identical — each carries a human touch.

Ethically Sourced

We are committed to responsible sourcing at every step. Our artisan partners receive fair wages, and our packaging is thoughtfully minimal — beautiful without waste.

How We Create

From sketch to skin.

Every Hira piece passes through four stages of careful creation before it reaches you.

01

Design

Every piece begins as a sketch — refined until it captures the perfect balance of tradition and modern sensibility.

02

Craft

Skilled artisans bring the design to life using time-honoured techniques passed down through generations of craftsmen.

03

Polish

Each piece is inspected, polished, and hallmarked to BIS standards before it earns the right to carry the Hira name.

04

Yours

Packaged with care and delivered to your door — ready to become part of your story and daily expression.
